Shekila Tibs

Description

this is ethiopian traditional food named shekila tibs made by using meat and other ingredients

Ingredients:

  • 1 lb beef (tenderloin or sirloin), cut into small cubes
  • 2 tablespoons Niter Kibbeh (Ethiopian spiced clarified butter) or cooking oil
  • 1 large red onion, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 to 3 jalapeños or green chilies, sliced
  • 1 tablespoon garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon fresh ginger, minced
  • 1 sprig fresh rosemary
  • 1 tablespoon Awaze paste or Berbere (optional, for heat)
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Heat a large pan or skillet over high heat. If you have a traditional clay Shekila dish, start preheating it over hot coals.
  2. Add the beef cubes to the dry, hot pan and sear them quickly to lock in the juices.
  3. Once the meat is browned on the outside, add the Niter Kibbeh and sliced onions. Sauté for 2-3 minutes.
  4. Stir in the chopped tomatoes, jalapeños, garlic, and ginger. Cook for another 2-3 minutes, keeping the meat tender.
  5. Toss in the sprig of fresh rosemary, and season with salt, black pepper, and Awaze if using.
  6. Transfer the sizzling mixture to the preheated clay Shekila dish over the coals and serve immediately.
